U.S. Fuel Surcharges

The fuel surcharge percentage for FedEx Express® services (excluding U.S. Domestic Express Freight) and the charge per pound for U.S. Domestic Express Freight services are subject to weekly adjustment based on the weekly published U.S. Gulf Coast (USGC) spot price for a gallon of kerosene-type jet fuel.*

The fuel surcharge percentage for FedEx Ground® services is subject to weekly adjustment based on the weekly published national U.S. on-highway average price for a gallon of diesel fuel.*

The fuel surcharge percentage for FedEx Freight® services is based on the weekly published national U.S. on-highway average price for a gallon of diesel fuel.

*Notwithstanding any information provided herein, the customer agreement is the final authority for all matters related to the customer’s fuel surcharge calculation and the frequency of the calculation adjustment

*If the fuel price posted on eia.gov is received after Monday, then the effective date and the FedEx Freight fuel surcharge displayed on this page will be delayed.

The U.S. Department of Energy fuel index is updated on the first business day of each week. The applicable surcharge will become effective on the Wednesday immediately following for rating purposes.

The fuel surcharge rate for FedEx Freight services applies as specified in Item 570 of the FXF 100 Series Rules Tariff.

Changes to the FedEx Express and FedEx Ground fuel surcharges will be applied effective each Monday. The surcharge percentage will be reflected on your FedEx invoice. Information on the fuel surcharge for each week will be available the Friday before the surcharge is applicable.

  • For FedEx Express, see the USGC index for the spot price per gallon of kerosene-type jet fuel.
  • For FedEx Ground, see the on-highway index for the price of a gallon of diesel fuel.
  • The prices in these indexes are published by the U.S. Department of Energy and FedEx is not responsible for the information provided.

Changes to the FedEx Freight fuel surcharge will be applied weekly. The surcharge will be reflected on your FedEx invoice.

  • For FedEx Freight, see the on-highway index for the price of a gallon of diesel fuel.

See previous FedEx Express and FedEx Ground fuel surcharges.

See previous FedEx Freight fuel surcharges.

For FedEx Express shipments, excluding shipments where a weight-based calculation is used, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the net package rate plus the following applicable surcharges:  additional handling, additional handling – non-stackable, broker select option fee, courier pickup, delivery area surcharge, delivery reattempt, extended service area delivery, extended service area pickup, FedEx® Collect on Delivery, FedEx® Delivery Signature Options, metro service area delivery, metro service area pickup, international out-of-delivery-area, international out-of-pickup-area, FedEx International Premium® pickup, oversize, peak – additional handling, peak — oversize, peak-residential, peak surcharge, residential delivery, residential pickup, return on-call pickup, FedEx ExpressTag®, Saturday delivery and Saturday pickup, and any temporary surcharge(s) currently in effect. This list may be amended or augmented without notice. For FedEx Express shipments where a weight-based calculation is used, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the billable weight of the shipment. Billable weight may include additional deficit weight when applicable.

For FedEx Ground shipments, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the net package rate plus the following applicable surcharges: additional handling, call tag, delivery area surcharge, FedEx® Delivery Signature Options, FedEx Ground® C.O.D., FedEx Ground® Electronic C.O.D., FedEx Home Delivery® Convenient Delivery Options, Northern Canada surcharge, on-call pickup, oversize, peak – additional handling, peak — ground unauthorized, peak — oversize, peak-residential, unauthorized, residential delivery, return on-call pickup and any temporary surcharge(s) currently in effect. This list may be amended or augmented without notice.

For FedEx Freight shipments, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on all rates and charges for linehaul transportation, and other services which consume fuel, in tariffs or contracts making reference to Item 570 of the FXF 100 Series Rules Tariff.
