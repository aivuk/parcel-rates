2022 FedEx shipping rate changes

We regularly evaluate our shipping rates and fees and adjust them when needed. This enables us to keep investing in our business so we can continue delivering the shipping solutions you need and the excellent service you’ve come to expect from us.

Here are the details of recent changes to list rates and fees, along with information on other factors that might affect your shipping rates.

FedEx regularly reviews its fuel surcharge and will update tables for FedEx Express, FedEx Ground, and FedEx Freight shipments beginning April 4, 2022.

This change will allow FedEx to provide customers the best service possible as rising fuel costs add to the cost to serve. Updated amounts can be found here.

  • FedEx Express, FedEx Ground, FedEx Ground® Economy, and FedEx Freight rates will increase.
  • There will be changes to shipping surcharges and fees that may apply to your shipment and affect your total shipping rate.
  • There will be changes to the Delivery Area Surcharge ZIP code lists for FedEx Express and FedEx Ground. View the Delivery Area Surcharge ZIP code changes PDF.

More delivery time options for your international customers

Beginning Jan. 3, 2022, FedEx will offer customers who ship internationally an additional option for time-definite deliveries in one to three business days. With the new FedEx International Priority® Express service, customers will be able to schedule delivery by noon to select destinations. Early morning delivery will continue to be available through the FedEx International First® service and end of day delivery to more than 220 countries will continue to be offered through the FedEx International Priority® service.

To use this new service, customers will need the latest version of the FedEx-enabled automation. Customers who have questions about their current automation solution should contact the Technical Support team at 1.877.339.2774 or reach out to their sales professional.

The following FedEx Express, FedEx Ground, FedEx Ground Economy and FedEx Freight surcharge changes will take place:

  • Effective Jan. 17, 2022, FedEx Freight will introduce a No Shipment Tendered surcharge that applies when a pickup is performed and no shipment is tendered to the carrier.
  • Effective Jan. 17, 2022, the International Out-of-Delivery-Area Surcharge and International Out-of-Pickup-Area Surcharge rates will be determined based on the corresponding tier of the ZIP code, postal code, or city of the shipment’s origin and/or destination location for International Express Freight and Parcel services.
  • Effective Jan. 17, 2022, a Delivery and Returns Surcharge will be assessed on packages that are delivered or returned using FedEx Ground Economy services.
  • Effective Jan. 24, 2022, the Additional Handling Surcharge and Oversize Charge rates for U.S. Express Package Services and U.S. Ground Services will be determined based on the shipment’s zone.
  • Details on these changes can be found here.

FedEx Freight continues to monitor market and network demands to provide our customers with the best possible service.

Effective Nov. 15, 2021, Item 525 Over Length and Item 530 Extreme Length surcharges will increase.

FedEx Freight zone-based rates

FedEx Freight zone-based rates are available for FedEx Freight® Priority and FedEx Freight® Economy services. Additional fees and surcharges may apply for fuel and other services.

To determine your FedEx Freight zone-based rate, you'll need:

  1. A FedEx Freight zone locator to find the zone for your shipment.
  2. FedEx Freight additional pickup and delivery charge ZIP code and postal code tier lists (if applicable):
  • Contiguous U.S. (PDF)
  • Contiguous U.S. (ASCII)
  • Canada (PDF)
  • Canada (ASCII)
  1. FedEx Freight zone-based rate tables (including pickup and delivery charges) for shipments. View 2022 FedEx Freight zone-based rate booklets:
  • Within the contiguous U.S.
  • From the contiguous U.S. to Canada
  • From Canada to the contiguous U.S.

Customers can also get zone-based rate information by:

  • Downloading the zone-based rate disks
    • FXF PZONE rates (for FedEx Freight Priority)
    • FXF EZONE rates (for FedEx Freight Economy)
  • Going to the Get Rates & Transit Times tool

Certain fees and surcharges and the FedEx Freight fuel surcharge may also apply.

To read PDF files, you'll need the free Adobe Reader or plug-in.

Answers to your questions about rate changes

Why are there seasonal peak surcharges during the holiday shipping season?

As the impact of COVID-19 continues to generate a surge in residential deliveries, we are entering this holiday peak season with extremely high demand for capacity and are experiencing increased operating costs across our network. We anticipate residential volume to continue to surge into the new year. ​These surcharges and fees will allow us to continue providing our customers with the best possible service during this challenging time.

When do the rate and fee changes take effect?

All new rate, surcharge and fee changes will take effect on Monday, Jan. 3, 2022, unless otherwise noted.

How can I get a current FedEx rate quote?

You can view standard list and retail rate tables by FedEx Express® and FedEx Ground® delivery service to see how much it costs to ship your package. You can also determine delivery zone by ZIP code or find out how to sign up your customers to access FedEx shipping rates directly from your website.

Calculate costs with FedEx Rate Tools

For these tools to function properly, you must disable your internet browser's pop-up blocker. 

How do electronic shipping solutions support the rate change?

  • FedEx Express and FedEx Ground rate changes will be available to all users of FedEx Ship Manager® at fedex.com and FedEx Web Services.
  • FedEx Express and FedEx Ground rates will be downloaded to all versions of FedEx Ship Manager® and FedEx Ship Manager® Server on or after Jan. 3, 2022.
  • FedEx Freight rates will be available to specific versions of FedEx Ship Manager and FedEx Ship Manager Server.

To ensure your automation solution reflects the 2022 rate changes, you will need to be on the latest versions of software.
